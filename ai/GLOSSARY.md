# Glossary

AWG: Atmospheric Water Generator

MoistAirState: Immutable description of moist air.

Evaluate(): Calculates next state without mutation.

Commit(): Applies accepted state.

Humidity Ratio: kg water/kg dry air.

Dew Point: Temperature where condensation begins.

LDF: Linear Driving Force kinetic model.

COP: Coefficient of Performance.

SOC: Battery State of Charge.
