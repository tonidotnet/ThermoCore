# A/B/C Physical Test Protocol

## A --- Direct cooling

Ambient → cooling device → condensate.

## B --- Sensible-heating control

Ambient → heater/solar air heater → cooling device.

Purpose: verify that sensible heating alone does not increase humidity
ratio/dew point.

## C --- Sorbent regeneration

Ambient → silica-gel adsorption → regeneration → conditioned
high-dew-point stream → cooling device.

## Report

Inlet/outlet T, RH, dew point, humidity ratio, water, electrical Wh,
thermal input where measurable, L/kWh-electric, solar/thermal-normalized
yield and recovery fraction.

Stop if appliance inlet limits, safe TEC temperatures, icing or
electrical safety limits are exceeded.
