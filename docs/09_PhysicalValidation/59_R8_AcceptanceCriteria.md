# R8 Acceptance Criteria

R8 is complete when: - Mirabelle has ≥3 repeatable baseline runs; -
Siguro has ≥3 repeatable baseline runs; - water mass and electrical
energy are measured; - inlet/outlet psychrometric state is logged; - ≥1
controlled high-dew-point comparison exists; - A/B/C protocol is
attempted and documented; - campaign data imports into ThermoCore; -
calibration and holdout reports exist; - model-vs-measurement errors are
quantified; - existing simulation regressions remain intact.

A quantified negative result is a valid R8 result.
