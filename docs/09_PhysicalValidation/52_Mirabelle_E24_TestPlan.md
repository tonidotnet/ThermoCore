# Mirabelle E24 Test Plan

Known: 12 V, 48 W appliance rating, climate class N, type
`7706 11000000`.

## M0 --- Non-invasive baseline

Measure DC V/I/W/Wh, internal T/RH, ambient T/RH, cold surface
temperature, hot exhaust/heat-sink temperature and transient response.

## M1 --- Condensation test

Provide a controlled humid-air path over a cold internal surface and
collect condensate without exposing electronics to water.

Record inlet dew point, cold surface, water mass, Wh and duration.

## M2 --- Internal identification

Only after baseline tests, open the appliance if practical. Photograph
and record TEC marking/dimensions, fans, heat sinks, thermal interface
and wiring.

**Do not infer TEC1-12704/12705/12706 from the 48 W appliance rating.**
Fan losses mean appliance input is not TEC Qmax or TEC electrical power.
