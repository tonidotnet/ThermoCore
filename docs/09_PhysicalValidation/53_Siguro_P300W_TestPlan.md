# Siguro SGR-DH-P300W Test Plan

Manufacturer reference: 330 W input, up to 20 L/day, 6.5 L tank,
continuous drainage, two fan speeds, anti-freeze/auto-defrost.

## S0 --- Room baseline

Continuous mode. Log room/inlet/outlet T/RH, power/Wh, water mass, fan
mode and detectable compressor cycling.

## S1 --- Controlled chamber

Prefer several conditions around 20/25/30 °C and 40/50/60/70% RH where
practical.

## S2 --- High-dew-point stream

Condition sorbent-regeneration air to remain inside the appliance inlet
limits while preserving high humidity ratio.

Compare direct ambient vs high-dew-point operation.

## Primary KPI

`Wh/L = accumulated electrical Wh / collected litres`.

The catalog 20 L/day is reference metadata, never measured campaign
output.
